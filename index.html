About the studio

Photography built around how Zambian weddings actually unfold.

Chola Chela Photography was built on a simple observation: a Zambian wedding isn't one event, it's several — each with its own dress code, its own choreography, and its own kind of chaos. Every package on this site is priced and built around a specific ceremony, not a generic "wedding day."

Every booking includes full-day coverage, a photo session with the couple or celebrant, and a private online gallery for delivery — no CDs, no waiting at the studio.
